## Kripke semantics for TraceMind pipelines

This verifier treats a `Plan` from `tm.pipeline.engine` as a Kripke structure and explores it with bounded BFS.

- **State**: `(store, pending, done, events)` where `store` is a key→value dict, `pending` is the queue of step names, `done` tracks which steps have been executed at least once, and `events` records executed steps. A state is *terminal* when no enabled successors exist.
- **Enabled step**: a pending step whose `reads` are all present in `store`. Executing a step removes one pending instance, marks it as `done`, writes its `writes` keys into the store, **removes its `clears` keys from the store**, appends an event, and enqueues any rule steps whose trigger selectors match the newly written paths. Multiple enabled steps branch nondeterministically.
- **Non-monotone facts (`clears`)**: a step may declare `clears: [key, ...]` to *remove* facts, so `has(key)` can go true→false (lock release, throttle off, un-quarantine, lease expiry). `writes` apply before `clears`, so a key in both ends up cleared. The store still ranges over the finite powerset `2^keys`, so exploration stays decidable and terminating (in `store` hash mode the reachable store set is finite even for a set/clear cycle). `clears` only affects the `meta.verify` transition system; runtime step execution ignores it.
- **Hash modes**: `full` hashes store + pending + done + events; `store` hashes only the store (coarser deduplication that merges scheduling variations).
- **Exploration**: breadth‑first up to `--depth N`, deduplicating by the chosen hash. Deadlocks are states with pending work but no enabled successors.
- **Invariants**: boolean formulas over predicates `Has(key)`, `Pending(step)`, `Done(step)`, `Terminal`. They must hold in every reachable state.
- **CTL subset**: `EX`, `EF`, `AF`, `EG`, `AG`, plus the binary Until operators `EU(p, q)` / `AU(p, q)` (textbook `E[p U q]` / `A[p U q]`; written function-call style because `[...]` collides with predicate-name tokenization). With `EX`, `EU`, `EG` this is the adequate CTL set. Plus boolean connectives. Formulas are evaluated on the bounded graph rooted at the initial state, and counterexample paths are reconstructed from BFS predecessors. `EU(true, q) ≡ EF q` and `AU(true, q) ≡ AF q`. Until is outside the sound compositional fragment, so such formulas fall back to monolithic verification.
- **Limitations**: bounded depth may miss longer counterexamples; coarse `store` hashing may merge states with different queues; step functions are abstracted to read/write effects only.
